What does product(A, B) do?

Think of it like nested loops
for a in A:
    for b in B:
        print((a, b))

What does * in print(*product(A, B)) mean?
print(product(A, B))

would print the product object itself.

The * unpacks all the tuples:

print(*( (1,3), (1,4), (2,3), (2,4) ))

which gives:

(1, 3) (1, 4) (2, 3) (2, 4)
